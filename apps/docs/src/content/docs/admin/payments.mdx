title: Payment configuration
description: Configure platform payment gateways separately from customer billing.
---

**Admin → Payment gateways** configures platform payment integrations. Customer balance, top-ups and transactions are in **Settings → Billing** and **Settings → Payments**; customers should not need merchant credentials to pay.

## Configure EPay

1. Open **Payment gateways** and inspect each integration's configured status.
2. Choose **Configure** for EPay.
3. Enter the merchant ID, gateway URL, notification URL and return URL supplied for your deployment. Enter the merchant signing key privately.
4. Choose the supported Alipay or WeChat Pay methods, and enable the integration when its configuration is ready.
5. Save, then reopen the configuration to confirm the stored state. Reads indicate whether a key exists rather than returning the saved key.

Configuration saves use a revision. Reload after a stale-revision conflict rather than overwriting another administrator's changes. Keep signing secrets out of source control, public files, screenshots and logs.

The supported gateway inventory can include integrations configured outside this dialog. A listed integration is not a promise that the dashboard can edit it; the current form edits EPay. See the generated [EPay configuration operation](/docs/api/rest/saveplatformepayconfiguration/) for exact fields and authorization.

## Verify payment behavior separately

Configured and enabled status do not establish merchant activation or a successful top-up. Verify the actual deployment's signed checkout, notification handling and funding records before advertising a method as available. A return-page visit is not settlement evidence. Do not grant customer balance from an unverified callback or duplicate an already recorded payment.

External refunds and complete commercial settlement qualification have their own implementation boundaries; do not infer them from a working configuration save.
