---
title: Customer pricing
description: Publish customer tariffs independently of Supplier purchase rates.
---

Open **Admin → Customer pricing** to manage the prices charged to a customer's workspace. Customer tariffs are distinct from confidential Supplier offers and procurement rates.

## Publish a tariff

1. Choose the **company and workspace** in the pricing view.
2. Select the model and inspect its current customer tariff and publication history.
3. Set the supported currency, rates and applicable price categories. Use the form's declared billing units; do not substitute a Supplier purchase price.
4. Publish the change and confirm the saved revision. If publication is unconfirmed, reload its state before attempting another change.
5. Make a bounded request through that workspace and inspect reported usage and its customer charge.

Rates are versioned. Subsequent price changes must not reprice a completed request. Supported cache, reasoning, context-tier, per-request and minimum-charge fields depend on the pricing contract; fields in a form do not establish every model's usage-category support.

## Pricing is not spending capacity

Company funds or approved credit, workspace spending caps and API-key caps are separate controls. A customer tariff determines a charge; it does not top up a balance or grant credit. Applicable reservations count toward supported spending limits before dispatch.

Missing usage or unresolved customer pricing must stay explicit. Do not label an unknown charge as free or replace it with an upstream expense. Read [usage and billing](/docs/concepts/performance-and-cost/) and the generated [customer tariff operations](/docs/api/rest/listplatformcustomertariffs/) for the contract.

Video/media rate schedules are separate from text token tariffs. Qualify the concrete model and meter before promising a video price or settled charge.
