title: Usage and billing
description: Understand reported model usage, customer charges and spending limits.
---

Niu records requests handled by your gateway. Use request logs and usage reports to see which models, workspace keys and periods account for consumption. A token count is usage evidence; it is not a price by itself.

## Read a customer charge

A priced request uses the effective customer tariff and the usage categories reported for that request. Customer prices are separate from confidential Supplier purchase rates and platform margins. A later tariff change must not rewrite an earlier request's price history.

If usage or a charge cannot be established, it stays unresolved. An unknown amount is not zero and does not imply free inference. A failed response or a disconnected client does not by itself prove that an upstream request never ran.

Read [request logs](/docs/concepts/execution-observation/) for the available status, timing, token and customer-charge evidence. High consumption is a reason to investigate; it does not prove unnecessary work or counterfactual savings.

## Understand spending controls

| Layer | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Company funds and approved credit | Shared customer spending capacity |
| Workspace cap | Limits applicable consumption within a workspace |
| API-key cap | Limits consumption attributed to an application key |

For supported priced requests, outstanding reservations count alongside committed charges. Rotating a key must not silently reset its budget identity. Personal upstream-key use is separate from customer funds; the contract defines which controls apply.

Customer Billing and Payments show customer balances, charges and transactions. Administrators configure [customer tariffs](/docs/admin/pricing/) and [payment integrations](/docs/admin/payments/). A configured gateway or checkout return page is not evidence that funds were received.

## Video and media usage

Video metering and pricing use the supported model's declared units and schedule, rather than assuming text-token prices. A successful job, retained output availability and a settled charge are separate states. Follow the [video guide](/docs/reference/video/) and its model-specific limits.

For operating measurements rather than application billing, see [gateway load testing](/docs/guides/gateway-load-testing/).
