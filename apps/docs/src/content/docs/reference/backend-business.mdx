## Supplier configuration

Supplier identity owns independent credentials and model bindings. Provider names
identify technical adapters. Follow the
[configuration workflow](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/supplier-key-isolation-live.md),
[offer directory](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/supplier-offer-directory.md),
[configuration usage](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/supplier-configuration-usage.md)
and [settlement history](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/supplier-settlement-history.md).
Procurement rates and Supplier earnings are separate from customer charges.
Personal OpenRouter testing does not establish qualified commercial supply.

## Workspace keys

Keys inherit workspace model access and may narrow it. Spending, request rate,
token rate and unresolved-request concurrency are separate controls:

- [Customer spending caps](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/api-key-spending-limits.md)
- [Request-rate limits](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/api-key-request-rate-limits.md)
- [Estimated token budgets](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/api-key-token-rate-limits.md)
- [Concurrent request limits](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/api-key-concurrency-limits.md)

Rotation preserves the key's spending identity and applicable limits. Revocation
does not erase already dispatched work or its unresolved liability. Finite token
budgets require a supported bounded request shape; missing usage remains unknown.

## Customer money and recovery

Read the [payment integration inventory](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/payment-aggregators.md)
for supported adapters and installation availability. Merchant activation is a
deployment concern; listing support does not grant balance. Browser returns do
not authorize funding. Approved credit is distinct from prepaid cash.

[Charge reconciliation](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/customer-charge-reconciliation.md)
and [supplemental token usage](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/token-category-reconciliation.md)
describe exact debits, unknown liabilities and original-price recovery. A recorded
usage receipt may precede settlement. Conflict errors must not be rendered as
zero usage or a successful financial operation.

## Video and invoices

The [media invoice contract](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/media-invoice-integration.md)
explains durable jobs, result recovery, billing states and invoice boundaries.
A completed personal video may retain null customer charges. Customer-priced media
requires supported measured usage and pinned pricing; requested duration or
upstream expense must not be substituted for a customer charge.

Positive customer media invoices and nonempty commercial Supplier settlement
remain unverified. Actual personal video recovery and mixed personal-video /
customer-text invoicing do not establish those financial paths.

## Current measured text paths

Pinned backend `e655a37` completed 600 real buffered requests across eight keys
at five scheduled requests per second and 120 real structured streams at two
scheduled requests per second. Independent raw-response and reopened-database
checks matched usage, route attribution and every exact debit, with no retained
customer reservation. These measurements use personal upstream access and
explicit internal prices/credit.

| Workload | Completed | Completion throughput | Client full round-trip p95 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Buffered Chat | 600 | 4.956 requests/second | 1,742.35 ms |
| Structured streaming Chat | 120 | 1.975 requests/second | 1,902.23 ms |

The streaming first-content p95 was 1,626.47 ms. All times include upstream work;
these runs do not establish Gateway-only latency, maximum capacity or complete
release readiness. See the
[buffered evidence](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/mixed-key-sustained-text.md)
and [streaming evidence](https://github.com/niu-io/niu/blob/main/docs/reference/published-route-stream-load.md)
for workload definitions and measurement limits.
