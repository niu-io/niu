# Detailed first-release acceptance requirements

These requirements supplement [the release checklist](first-release.md). They preserve the agreed F05/F08/F09 design and verification scope; they are not implementation status. Product direction remains in [product focus](../product/product-focus.md).

## Prepaid account balance and approved credit — F05

The default customer payment journey is company account balance in the global Settings → Billing & payments section, suitable for Chinese companies: view balance, top up, inspect consumption and receive low-balance warnings. Invoice generation/payment is not the default spending workflow. Statements and any required invoice documents are secondary records, not an additional source of funds. Do not require a credit card or claim a payment channel is supported before integrating and qualifying it.

- **Account ledger:** use an authoritative durable billing account with explicit ownership and currency. Billing and payment configuration belongs to the company account, not individual workspaces. Workspaces retain usage attribution and spending/key limits, and charge the designated shared account; pooled account capacity must not become independent balances duplicated per key or workspace. Use exact monetary units, immutable customer price revisions and traceable credits, deductions, refunds and adjustments. Never mix Supplier expenses into customer balances.
- **Funding:** credit only verified settled payments or authorized adjustments. Payment callbacks and retries are authenticated and idempotent; pending or failed top-ups grant no capacity. Reconcile refunds and reversals without erasing the original transaction or duplicating funds.
- **Credit and admission:** the approved credit limit defaults to zero and may be changed only by authorized administration. Available capacity equals posted balance plus approved credit limit minus outstanding reservations. Atomically reserve a defensible maximum liability before paid dispatch, accounting for concurrent requests, retries and uncertain outcomes. Reject requests that cannot be funded; zero capacity admits no paid request. Settle actual customer charges once and release unused reservations. Unknown liabilities remain reserved until reconciled. If a route cannot bound liability, it cannot be offered under guaranteed prepaid enforcement.
- **Negative balances:** approved credit permits posted balance below zero down to the approved limit. Exhausted capacity suspends new paid inference; it does not remove authentication, billing history or top-up access. A settled overrun remains an accurate debt and triggers suspension/investigation; do not clamp the balance or advertise uncontrolled overdraft as credit. Credit-limit reductions must account for existing reservations and liabilities.
- **Warnings and recovery:** configure a meaningful low-balance threshold in the account currency. Show balance, available capacity and funding action clearly; deliver supported opted-in notifications with deduplication and recovery/re-arm behavior. Display negative balance and credit availability honestly. Verified funding or an approved credit change restores paid access only when sufficient capacity is available. Do not invent a fixed warning threshold or promise unimplemented notification channels.

Acceptance requires a funded request and matching balance deduction across Chat/API, Logs, Usage and Billing; zero-credit rejection without upstream dispatch; approved-credit spending into a negative balance; exhaustion and funding recovery; cross-workspace concurrent reservations against one account; duplicate/out-of-order payment callbacks; failed funding; refunds/reversals; cancellation, retries and uncertain settlement; credit-limit changes; warning crossing/recovery; restart durability; and negative authorization tests. Verify the global Settings billing section, transaction history paging/retry, account switching and company-only authorization at desktop and narrow widths. Preserve workspace attribution without independent workspace billing configuration. Existing invoice reconciliation evidence is historical accounting coverage and does not qualify this payment model.

## Gateway Activity and request observability — F08

Activity summarizes requests handled by Niu; Logs explain individual requests. Optional task/session metadata groups authorized gateway requests without claiming a complete agent execution. Full agent observability belongs to Supen. External agent logs, tool events and subscription usage imports are outside this release; existing historical observations must not be automatically deleted.

- **Scope and access:** global Activity includes only workspaces the signed-in user may read. Workspace views, request details, payloads and exports enforce the same authorization on the backend. Administrator access must not expose procurement data through customer surfaces.
- **Consumption:** show request counts, reported token categories and customer charges over the complete selected interval. Preserve unknown usage and charges, category overlap and measurement coverage. Model/key/time breakdowns open matching Logs with the same scope and filters; pagination must not change aggregate totals.
- **Diagnosis:** retain meaningful model/key names, outcome, failure or finish reason, retries and measured latency. Request details provide an aligned timing waterfall and consented retained payloads where available. Distinguish routing, waiting and generation only when recorded measurements support them. Do not infer causality, productivity, wasted work or complete task duration from gateway requests.
- **Durability and data controls:** request metadata, accounting and retained content remain authoritative on the backend across reload, another browser and server restart. Payload retention/deletion must not destroy required accounting metadata; revoked access must deny both details and downloads. Content and identifiers follow the shared privacy and commercial boundaries.
- **Responsive investigation:** use the established Activity → filtered Logs → request detail journey. Verify readable filters, timing, content inspection and return navigation at desktop and narrow widths. Follow the shared reference-led design rules; do not add unrelated agent-management destinations.

Acceptance requires a real authorized request and matching Activity/Logs/customer billing records; full-range model/key/period drilldowns and exports; known and unknown timing/usage cases; failed and retried requests; task correlation limited to gateway observations; negative cross-workspace access; revoked-session/key behavior; retention/deletion; and restart durability. Qualify text and asynchronous video diagnosis separately, including uncertainty and historical pricing. Local fixtures qualify their tested contracts only, not live Supplier behavior or the complete F08 gate.

## Workflow review requirements

The [workflow delivery plan](../product/workflow-delivery-plan.md) is part of this release scope. It expands F02–F10 with commercial identity/pricing, a focused qualified catalog, server-wide consumption analysis, durable multi-turn Chat, gateway request investigation and usable mobile navigation. Follow its delivery order and record each finding as reproduced, fixed, already resolved or blocked. Preserve existing review changes; tests and configuration gaps alone do not establish feature acceptance.

## Workspace Guardrails — F09

Workspace Guardrails are included in this release plan. Earlier backlog scheduling does not establish completion or exclude these requirements. Deliver in stages: policy composition and deterministic local input controls; explicitly configured external detectors and non-streaming output checks; then qualified streaming inspection. Unsupported operations and modes must remain explicit throughout. Each stage requires its own fresh evidence, and existing key grants or payload redaction alone do not qualify Guardrails.

- **Policy management:** versioned workspace defaults and key assignments, role-checked CRUD, optimistic revisions, draft validation, effective-policy/route eligibility preview, activation and rollback. Define `inherit`, `allow_all`, `allow_list` and `deny_all`; intersect model/Provider grants and credential scopes, and deny an empty intersection. Children and request overrides may tighten mandatory restrictions but never weaken them. Apply retention/region requirements on every route, retry and fallback; distinguish verified evidence, accepted attestations and unknown classifications. Bind decisions to workspace, request, attempt, route and policy revision. Extend member assignments only after identity and budget ownership are defined.
- **Local input controls:** bounded secret/PII presets and custom regex with block/redact for an explicit textual Chat/Responses subset. Publish extraction and coverage for roles, content parts, prompts, tool arguments/results, Embeddings, files, encoded/multimodal data, candidate outputs, visible reasoning and unknown events. Preserve Unicode and JSON structure; revalidate transformed requests and recompute size/cost bounds. Unsupported content must be rejected when a mandatory policy cannot inspect it. Required checks run before dispatch, with zero upstream calls on blocked or indeterminate input. Optional policies must not complicate simple initial access. Pattern-based injection detection is best effort, never a universal prevention claim.
- **Detector adapters:** optional NLP, moderation and injection classifiers use authenticated, versioned and bounded interfaces. Required timeout, overload, unavailable service or malformed verdict is indeterminate and fails closed; observe-only mode is explicitly non-enforcing. Configuration declares recipients, content sent, credentials, retention, region, timeout, concurrency and costs. Apply egress/SSRF and redirect controls; never forward client authorization. Detector privacy is independent of inference Provider privacy. No speculative inference dispatch for pre-egress protection.
- **Output modes:** distinguish observe-only, bounded inspection before each supported chunk is released, and bounded full-output inspection. Reject incompatible streaming or oversized output, or buffer within declared bounds; never silently downgrade. Delivered bytes cannot be recalled, and windowed inspection is not full-response semantic coverage. Define protocol-correct termination, cancellation and settlement for blocked output, including incurred detector/Provider charges. Never retry on a less restricted route or change Providers after response commitment. Inspecting tool-call text does not govern tool execution outside Niu.
- **Budget semantics:** reuse the authoritative ledger with explicit subjects, currency, reset timezone and period. Distinguish independent key/member allowances from pooled balances. Hard caps require concurrency-safe reservations and bounded costs covering retries, uncertain liabilities and detector/Provider charges. If these prerequisites are unavailable, explicitly defer hard budget caps rather than introduce a second counter or advertise strict enforcement.
- **Dashboard and audit:** a Guardrails area under workspace settings provides presets, coverage/mode labels, synthetic tests and effective-policy/impact preview before activation. Safe audit metadata includes policy/detector versions, stage, outcome, reason code, inspected coverage, duration and request/attempt correlation. Raw matches, originals, secrets and detector responses are excluded by default from all logs, traces, errors and preview surfaces. Preserve public enforcement, basic isolation, local filters and adapter contracts without private-source dependencies; confirm any advanced edition boundary before implementation. Local filters require no new external service, proxy or application domain. Pin selectively reused modules/dependencies and verify licenses.

Acceptance requires cross-workspace and weakening/bypass tests; invalid/conflicting updates leaving the active revision intact; concurrent activation/reassignment/deletion tests; a fake upstream proving zero dispatch for required denials; transformed protocol-object assertions; and table-driven Unicode, nested JSON, role/tool, unsupported-content, invalid-pattern, oversized-input, timeout/outage and false-positive/negative fixtures. External adapters require SSRF, redirect/proxy, credential-isolation, forged/oversized-result, concurrency and cancellation tests. Output qualification covers held chunks, fragmented UTF-8/SSE/tool arguments, cross-chunk patterns, malformed events, buffer exhaustion, disconnects and mid-stream blocks. Fixture secrets must be absent across every sink. Shipping budgets require concurrent rollover/retry/liability reconciliation without double charging.

Measure added P50/P95/P99 latency, TTFT, throughput, CPU/RSS, detector cost, detection errors and failure behavior under fixed payload/concurrency for disabled, local, external, windowed and buffered modes. Establish rollout thresholds from measurements; make no unmeasured overhead claim. A packaged test and desktop/narrow dashboard walkthrough must demonstrate creation, preview, assignment, denial/redaction, authorized audit and rollback, with pinned artifacts and documented coverage/failure semantics. Use actual OpenRouter Guardrails interactions as the UI reference while retaining Niu's components and brand.
