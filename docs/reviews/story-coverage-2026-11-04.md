## What these observations imply

The reviewed implementation supplies useful primitives, but a competitive claim requires connected outcomes. The recurrent root is a break between resource setup, authorized execution, evidence and recovery. A successful isolated operation can coexist with a blocked first use, a misleading operational state or an unbounded expense path.

The repair direction remains a coherent user-task contract: reveal real prerequisites before setup, authorize named scope and a truthful financial basis, prepare ordinary access automatically, verify what actually ran, retain enough authorized evidence to resume, and keep the responsible owner informed until current-configuration recovery is proven. Human trial/investigation surfaces and direct agent operations need the same facts and boundaries. Optional commercial administration must retain its own audience and must not become the default onboarding burden.

Competitive references in the reset review establish baseline expectations, not measured superiority. No quantitative improvement claim is justified until equivalent tasks are operated with comparable starting conditions. The destination dev entry must be restored before its connected browser stories can be accepted.
