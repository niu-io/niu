## Provider adapters own price discovery

Provider-specific behavior belongs to a backend Provider adapter, separate from the existing inference protocol adapters. OpenAI-compatible transport does not establish common pricing semantics: FastRouter, BabelArk, Qiniu and DeepSeek require independent service adapters even when their requests share a wire format.

A Provider adapter should expose model discovery, price discovery, source/version timestamps, capability coverage and supported account usage/bill reconciliation. Account billing reads require separately established authentication and scope. Adapters normalize monetary currency, quantity units, cache read/write, reasoning, group discounts, tiers and time windows only where supported by evidence. Preserve the raw quote provenance and the normalization version for review and historical estimates.

The shared result distinguishes supported/partial/unavailable discovery, advertised unit quotes, effective account quotes and Provider-reported expenses. Each adapter owns its documented API, explicitly qualified console endpoint, or validated central official-source catalog fallback. Price absence is a result, not permission to treat an API-compatible model as free. Neither a Provider adapter nor a quote fetch publishes a customer selling tariff.

Use explicit saved Provider service identity; do not infer an account's rate plan or pricing adapter from its model name or protocol compatibility. Native coding-agent integrations, MCP, SDKs and dashboard surfaces consume the same backend contract. Keep account-specific credentials and confidential procurement out of public catalog responses.

## Integration policy

Prefer documented, machine-readable Provider quote feeds. For console-internal endpoints, record the observed schema and authorization limit and treat schema changes as an adapter failure, not a reason to invent a price. Never request a broader management credential merely to fill a missing price without an explicit authorization contract.

When a Provider has no stable quote API, a centrally maintained, versioned official-source price catalog can avoid requiring each installation to enter every model price. An automated page adapter needs validation, drift detection, source timestamps and failure behavior; its existence is not established by this audit. Preserve manual overrides separately and show which source takes precedence.

Missing, stale, incomplete, subscription-specific and currency-incompatible price evidence must remain explicit. An empty pricing response is not free inventory. Preserve quote versions used for estimates; distinguish those estimates from Provider-reported costs and Niu customer charges. Do not infer an exact rate by dividing rounded aggregate bills by token totals. Do not flatten time-of-day, cache, tier, image, audio or video pricing into one universal token rate.

## Verification on 2026-11-02

Provider adapters preserve a normalization version and SHA-256 digest after a bounded source read. Captured versions are immutable; estimates require their original revision and retain currency, unknown meters and unsupported dimensions. Pure parsing does not invent an observation timestamp. BabelArk group multipliers and model group eligibility remain native evidence, with no automatic claim about an inference key's effective group or monetary basis.

The downloaded official DeepSeek Markdown and Qiniu SSR JSON are tested through the production normalizers in addition to synthetic schema-drift fixtures. Local gateway retrieval remains separately unverified where the development network resolves a public host into the reserved `198.18.0.0/15` range. The gateway correctly rejects that destination. Do not weaken endpoint safety, report a parser-only check as end-to-end transport acceptance, or replace an unavailable quote with zero.

## Adapter capability and fixture verification on 2026-11-06

All five adapters report `adapter_version: "2026-11-06.1"`, capabilities and the source-implied `price_currency` (or `null` when unknown). Normalized evidence is version 2 and additionally exposes `source_version` (a Provider-published feed version when present), `discounts` (batch variants, group multipliers, volume tiers, off-peak rules and free quotas), `ambiguities` (currency/unit/group/condition gaps) and a `recovery` code. None of these fields is a customer tariff or an account-specific effective price.
