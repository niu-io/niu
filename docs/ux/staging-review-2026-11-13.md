## Product boundary

Niu supplies scoped model information, authorized management controls, and inspectable results for agents and people. The agent or user chooses task goals and judges outcomes. The customer journey reviewed here is discover a model, inspect access and prices, choose or create a workspace API key, use Generations, inspect requests and charges, and recover from unavailable services. Provider procurement and confidential costs are outside this customer journey. This boundary follows `docs/product/agent-oriented-principles.md`; existing pages do not by themselves establish that every capability is complete.
