# Product workflow delivery plan

This plan expands the first-release acceptance matrix with the findings from the October 2026 dashboard review. It is an implementation checklist, not a claim that reviewed capabilities are missing or fixed. Reproduce each finding against the current worktree before changing it. The product-focus and first-release documents remain authoritative.

## Supplier credential coverage — F03

- Add a key to an existing Supplier without creating another Supplier business. Support one key serving multiple models and multiple keys with independent model subsets.
- Qualify the same workflow with a non-OpenRouter Supplier. Cover one model and several models per key; adding a Supplier must not require an OpenRouter account or OpenRouter-specific catalog discovery. Document adapter limitations explicitly.
- Keep credentials encrypted and scoped to their configuration; rotate or disable one without changing the others. Preserve Supplier ownership and agreed offers.
- Verify both configurations through onboarding, model mapping, rate setup and inference. Do not infer entitlement from a model catalog.
- The add dialog supports an existing Supplier and an independent API key name. Qualification still requires a complete rendered multi-key workflow, including saved ownership, mappings, rates and real inference.

## Delivery order

Deliver in the following dependency order; video is mandatory first-release scope:

1. Supplier identity, independent credentials, mappings, qualified capabilities and rates; immutable customer tariffs and shared-account admission. Include the targeted Seedance model/schema/meter mechanisms (V01, V04; M01–M05).
2. Durable text-input video create/query APIs, request validation and original-account authorization; bounded recovery and exactly-once settlement before dependent UI. Qualify callbacks separately, preserve uncertain outcomes, and expose customer-safe historical charge explanations (V02, V05, V06, V13, V14, V19, V20; M05–M08).
3. Authorized result retrieval and the complete text-to-video customer journey: safe preview/download, retention/deletion, saved history, measured timings and customer Logs/Usage/Billing at desktop and narrow widths (V07, V14–V16).
4. Inspection before image/reference generation inputs, then ordinary assets, readiness-aware reusable references, explicit-consent liveness and separate portrait approval; qualify channel differences and account isolation (V03, V08–V12, V15, V18, V21).
5. Matching API/SDK/docs with each increment, followed by authorized live qualification and packaged install/restart/restore/upgrade evidence (V17). Complete remaining in-scope F01–F10 workflows.

Follow the [immediate implementation queue](../releases/first-release.md#immediate-implementation-queue): Supplier prerequisites → one reconciled text-to-video lifecycle → safe results and the complete customer journey → reference media/assets/verification → release qualification. The [saved-result dashboard increment](../releases/video-result-dashboard-verification.md) has scoped local evidence. Audit Supplier configuration through estimate/submission, recovery, results and customer Logs/Usage/Billing as one workflow; fix the first unmet dependency before starting dependent media screens. Qualify a concrete video Supplier/model alongside implementation. If live qualification is unavailable, continue local integration, authorization/recovery checks and required media-safety mechanisms without claiming live acceptance.

Extend the locally verified capability, output/meter, media-offer, customer tariff, estimate, durable job and result-storage foundations instead of rebuilding them. Their checkpoints do not qualify a live channel. Preserve historical job prices and keep purchase rates private. Assets and real-person verification retain their mandatory separate acceptance gates; this ordering does not defer them beyond the first release.

Do not defer implementing required mechanisms because a concrete channel is unqualified. Keep that channel unavailable until its exact contract and authorized live behavior are evidenced. Verify existing foundations before extending them, and apply responsive shared-layout fixes alongside each affected workflow.

External agent observability is outside Niu’s scope; qualify gateway Activity and Logs instead.

For the OpenRouter test Supplier, use its current public model rates as the agreed test rates. Do not assume a discount. On October 2, 2026, the development configuration was associated with its Supplier identity and 25 model offers were saved and checked against the live OpenRouter catalog's input/output token prices. This verifies saved test rates only; customer price revisions, live protocol qualification and the rendered Supplier workflow remain separate acceptance checks.

### Required Seedance model and billing mechanism — F03, F04, F05, F06

**Required product capability; internal calculation, persistence, customer tariff selection and text-input video dispatch/recovery/settlement foundations implemented with local fixtures; complete media coverage and live route qualification pending.** Niu must support Seedance-class models and their metering/pricing mechanisms, including the targeted Seedance 2.5 and 2.0/Fast/Mini variants. Supplier qualification decides which concrete model/channel/rate is available; it does not replace or defer implementing the required mechanism. M01–M08 supplement the existing V01/V04/V13 acceptance rows and remain pending, independently of current promotional prices. The [metering checkpoint](../releases/video-metering-foundation-verification.md) verifies internal exact calculations, scoped pricing/usage, shared capacity and settlement fixtures; it does not qualify a video route or Provider.

[Official Seedance pricing](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/ark/model-pricing?lang=en&redirect=1) describes video-token billing: estimated tokens = (input reference-video seconds + output seconds) × output width × output height × output frame rate / 1024. Accurate metering uses the task's `usage.completion_tokens`; [the official task-query contract](https://docs.volcengine.com/docs/ark/get-video-generation-task-api?lang=zh) is the usage reference. Reference-video inputs have minimum-token rules; model/version, resolution and reference-video presence select rates. Published per-second prices are specification-based equivalents. `total_tokens == completion_tokens` does not mean reference video is free, nor establish a text input/output split. Official successful-task charging and FastRouter's account/charging policies require separate qualification.
